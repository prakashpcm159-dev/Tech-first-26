OUR STORY

What is TechFest 26?

TechFest 26 is a college technology festival created to bring students, developers, creators and technology enthusiasts together.

The festival combines practical workshops, technology talks, competitions and networking opportunities in one exciting experience.

Everyone is welcome. Come with curiosity, learn something new and leave with something you built.
